8 portals, not one. That is what “government hub” really means for federal contractors. If you only watch SAM.gov, you will miss real work moving through vehicle portals and agency systems.

One takeaway you can use today: set a 3-level cadence. Daily, check SAM Contract Opportunities and any vehicle portals you hold, like eBuy or SEWP. Weekly, review awards in SAM Contract Data and USASpending to spot recompetes. Monthly, clear compliance in CPARS, eSRS, FSRS, and refresh SAM reps and certs. Tie this to thresholds in FAR 2.101, 5.201, 19.702, and 42.1502 so you know what must be posted and what stays inside vehicles.

We listed the exact hubs, thresholds, and a setup checklist in the post. For small teams, this avoids the two big traps, waiting on PIEE until after award and ignoring CPARS drafts. Read the guide, then tighten your cadence.
