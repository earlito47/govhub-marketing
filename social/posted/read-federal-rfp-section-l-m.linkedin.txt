90 percent of preventable proposal losses trace back to Section L and Section M. Here is the fix in one line: read L to comply, write to M to win.

A practical takeaway you can use today: build a two-column list before you write a word. Column 1 is every “shall” in Section L. Column 2 is the Section M factor it supports. Anything without a match is compliance only. Your outline mirrors M, and your page weights follow M’s stated importance. If it is LPTA under FAR 15.101-2, keep the technical tight. If it is a tradeoff under FAR 15.101-1, spend pages where M puts weight.

Ship early. 52.215-1(c)(3) late rule is brutal, and portals fail at the worst time.
