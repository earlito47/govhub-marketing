Most evaluators read “total awarded amount” as the current total award value, not the ceiling. In public data, that is USAspending’s Current Total Value of Award, which maps to FPDS’s Base and Exercised Options Value. The Potential Total you see is the ceiling, not guaranteed spend, and Total Obligations is the money actually put on contract.

Quick takeaway you can use today: for IDIQs and BPAs, never claim the parent’s ceiling as past performance. Use task or delivery order Current Total and Obligations instead. You will avoid double counting and the quiet credibility hit when reviewers check FPDS.
