10 names make up a huge share of federal prime obligations every year. You know most of them, but the only list that matters is the one filtered to your buyer, your NAICS, and this fiscal year.

Here is the move that saves hours: pull USAspending.gov’s Award Spending by Recipient, set FY 2023 or FY 2024 YTD, Prime Awards only, add your Agency and NAICS or PSC, sort by Obligations, export. Group by Parent Recipient to avoid double counting subsidiaries. Now you have a defensible top 10 for your market, not a generic poster.

Use that list to decide team vs compete, map vehicles, and find small business liaisons under FAR 52.219-9 when subcontracting plans apply.
