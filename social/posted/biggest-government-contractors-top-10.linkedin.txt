The top 10 federal contractors in FY2023, by prime obligations, were led by Lockheed Martin at about $44B. RTX, General Dynamics, Boeing, and Northrop rounded out the top five. McKesson, Leidos, Humana, Booz Allen, and HII completed the list.

One practical takeaway: do not chase a "top contractor" unless they hold the vehicle where your target task order will drop. Most of these dollars flow through IDIQs and GWACs under FAR 16.505 and GSA MAS under FAR 8.405. Verify the vehicle in USASpending, check CPARS for delivery risk, and confirm a real opportunity with dates before you burn cycles.
