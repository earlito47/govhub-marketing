48% of the federal proposals we review fail Section L in at least one place. Most teams use tools built for corporate questionnaires, then wonder why they blow page limits or miss musts.

If you are evaluating an RFPIO alternative for federal work, run this test: can it parse Section L and M from a PDF, build a true compliance matrix you can export, enforce page caps, and auto-fill SF 33 or SF 1449 with your SAM data? If not, you will still be doing the hard parts in Excel and Word under deadline.

One practical takeaway you can use today: start every bid by allocating a page budget per L instruction, then lock styles on day one. You will catch scope creep at Pink Team, not at midnight on delivery day.
