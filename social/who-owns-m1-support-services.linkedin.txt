Two minutes is all it takes to check who owns a federal contractor. For M1 Support Services, L.P., the current answer is simple. Privately held, and no highest-level owner reported in SAM.gov or on FPDS awards.

Useful takeaway: FAR 52.204-17 forces contractors to disclose Immediate Owner and Highest-Level Owner in SAM. If those fields are blank, there is no parent on record that the contracting officer will see. Cross-check USAspending Award Summary for any Parent Recipient. Save screenshots and paste a one-line status into your capture plan.

Why this matters: ownership affects affiliation risk under 13 CFR 121.103, potential OCI issues under FAR 9.5, and novation disruption under FAR 42.1204. Get it right before you team or ghost.
