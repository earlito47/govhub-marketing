Most RFPIO quotes for SMB federal contractors land between $15k and $50k per year for 10-25 users, plus $3k-$12k one-time for implementation. Fifty-user teams often see $60k-$120k.

The quiet driver is not the per-seat number. It is the base platform fee and add-ons like SSO and Salesforce. Ask the rep to mark what is included in the base and what is an add-on, then lock a renewal cap at 5% and get data export terms in writing.

One useful benchmark: if your team runs 18 proposals a year and saves 2 hours per person per proposal, a 25-user plan returns about $76k in labor. That clears a typical subscription.
