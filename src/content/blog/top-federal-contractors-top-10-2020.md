## Top 10 government contractors, how this list is built
If you care about the real Top 10, you need one method and you need to apply it the same way each time. We use USAspending Award Search, filter to Prime Awards only, select a fiscal year, set Place of Performance to All, and sort by Recipient, Total Obligations. This aligns to what most capture teams mean when they ask who the largest federal contractors are.

A few ground rules that avoid bad lists:

- Use prime obligations, not total contract value. Total contract value includes options that may never get exercised.
- Roll up parents and subs thoughtfully. For example, Raytheon Technologies includes Collins Aerospace and Pratt and Whitney subsidiaries. USAspending has a Parent DUNS to UEI mapping, but it is not perfect.
- Pick one fiscal year. Mixing FY and calendar year will burn your time and confuse your team.

## Largest federal contractors by agency share
Most of the Top 10 ride on DoD. If you mostly sell civilian, these names shift.

- DoD. Lockheed Martin, Raytheon Technologies, Boeing, General Dynamics, Northrop Grumman, L3Harris, HII, BAE Systems. DoD obligations typically account for more than 60 percent of total federal prime spend in a year.
- HHS. McKesson and other distributors grow with pharmacy and vaccine buys. Amounts spike in health emergencies, then normalize.
- VA. Oracle America Cerner for EHR, Leidos for T4NG, Accenture Federal Services, Booz Allen Hamilton. Large IT and health services share here.
- DHS. Leidos, General Dynamics IT, SAIC, Booz Allen, CACI. Services heavy, with IDIQs and BPAs that obligate steadily over time.

This matters for teaming. If you are an 8 a professional services firm targeting VA IT, you will not partner with Lockheed first. You will meet the VA primes who actually win in your NAICS.

## How to find the top government contractors yourself, step by step
You can pull a defensible Top 10 in under 10 minutes. No subscription tools needed.

1) Go to USAspending.gov. Click Award Search, then Advanced Search.
2) Under Time Period, pick Fiscal Year and select the year you want. Start with FY 2023.
3) Under Award Type, select Contracts only. Leave Loans, Grants, and Other unchecked.
4) Under Prime Awards, set the toggle to Prime Only. This excludes subcontracts.
5) Optionally under Agencies, select Department of Defense or a civilian department if you want an agency specific Top 10.
6) Click Submit Search.
7) On the results page, click the Award Amounts tab, then click the Recipients sub tab.
8) Sort by Sum of Obligations, Descending. Use the Recipient Type filter to exclude foreign governments if needed.
9) Click Download if you want a CSV. Keep columns for Recipient UEI, Parent UEI, and Sum of Obligations.
10) If you publish the list, add a footnote. Source USAspending Award Search, Contracts, Prime Awards, FY 20XX, downloaded on [today's date].

## What small and mid firms should actually do with this list
The Top 10 is not a trophy case. It is a routing map.

- Build your partner short list by agency and NAICS. If you sell 541512 at VA, your partner list likely starts with Leidos, Booz Allen, Accenture Federal Services, and SAIC, not Lockheed Martin.
- Check which vehicles the top primes use. Look at GWACs and IDIQs like CIO SP3, OASIS, Alliant, Seaport NxG. If your pipeline is full of task orders off a vehicle you do not hold, find the primes that do.
- Read their Subcontracting Plans when available. FAR 19.702 requires a Small Business Subcontracting Plan on noncommercial contracts that exceed $750,000, except for small business prime awardees. The clause is FAR 52.219 9. It drives measurable small business goals.
- Know the Limitations on Subcontracting. FAR 52.219 14 sets performance thresholds for set asides. For services, the prime small business must perform at least 50 percent of the cost of contract performance incurred for personnel. For supplies, 50 percent of the cost of manufacturing, with some exceptions. For general construction, 15 percent. For specialty trade construction, 25 percent.
- Expect flowdowns. Common ones include FAR 52.204 21 for basic safeguarding of covered contractor information systems, DFARS 252.204 7012 for cyber incident reporting and NIST SP 800 171, and FAR 52.204 10 for reporting first tier subawards in FSRS. If you cannot meet these, you will not get on the team, or you will be limited to low risk work.

## Worked example, one week plan to turn the Top 10 into pipeline
Here is a checklist you can run Monday to Friday without new tools.

Day 1. Pull the list
- Run the USAspending steps above for FY 2023 and for your target agency. Export both CSVs.
- Normalize name variants. Map RTX to Raytheon Technologies, HII to Huntington Ingalls Industries, and so on.

Day 2. Map to vehicles and NAICS
- For each top firm in your agency list, identify the vehicles they are active on that match your NAICS. Use SAM.gov, the agency vehicle site, and their contracts page.
- Write down the exact contract numbers and ordering period end dates.

Day 3. Find three live or upcoming task orders
- In SAM.gov and eBuy, filter by the vehicles and NAICS you listed. Grab three actions with a due date inside 90 days or recompetes inside 12 months.
- Note the incumbent, the evaluator office, and the CPARS trail if public. CPARS is required per FAR 42.1502 for past performance reporting above the simplified acquisition threshold.

Day 4. Make the ask
- Build a one page capability brief matched to those three actions. Lead with past performance and CPARS quotes, the labor categories you can staff, and your small business set aside status.
- Email the capture or partner contact on the prime. Make one ask for a 15 minute call tied to a specific task order. Do not send a generic capability statement first.

Day 5. Screen the opportunity
- On the call, confirm role fit and flowdowns. Ask about OCI risks per FAR 9.5 if you also support the same office in advisory work.
- If both sides agree to move forward, request the draft plan for key clauses and the path to an executed NDA and TAs. No NDA means no data and no internal sharing. That stalls teaming every time.

## Notes on accuracy, definitions, and why your list may not match mine
- USAspending uses UEI and Parent UEI linkages that change. Corporate mergers also move names. That is why you will see RTX instead of pre merger Raytheon and UTC.
- FPDS reports obligations at the contract action level. USAspending ingests that daily. Late mods can shift totals after year end. Always timestamp your pull.
- Some lists use total revenue, which is a different question. We focus on federal prime obligations, by fiscal year.

## Quick answers to common questions
- Do I need Deltek or similar to get this list. No. USAspending is enough for a Top 10. Subscription tools help with task order level intelligence and contacts.
- Should I chase the biggest primes first. Only if they own the vehicle and NAICS you need. Otherwise, second tier primes move faster and value your skills more.
- Does small business status matter to primes. Yes, in two ways. Subcontracting plan targets per FAR 52.219 9, and small business participation evaluation factors under FAR 15.304 when an RFP includes them.
