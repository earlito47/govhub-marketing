Every outreach email identifies GovHub and Linkshub Ltd., includes our postal address, and tells you how to opt out. Replying "no" or "unsubscribe" to any of them stops email from all of these domains.

If you receive an email that claims to be from GovHub but comes from a domain that is not on this page, it is not from us. Please forward it to support@govhub.online. "GovHub" is a common name, and other organizations use it for unrelated services; govhub.com, for example, is not ours.
