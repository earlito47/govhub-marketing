## 1. What we collect

**When you create and use an account**

- Name, work email address, company name, and optionally your role, phone number and website.
- Your login: a password (stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider), or your Google account if you sign in with Google.
- Your company profile: legal name, address, UEI, CAGE code, contact details, capabilities, past performance and similar business details you enter or that we look up for you from public sources such as SAM.gov.
- A signature image, signer name and title, only if you choose to add them so GovHub can sign generated documents.
- Billing details. Stripe collects your payment card and billing address; we receive your plan, payment status and a Stripe customer reference, not your card number.
- Support requests and the messages you send us.

**Content you upload or create ("Customer Content")**

Solicitations, attachments, capability statements, past performance, resumes and key personnel details, pricing, drafts, forms, and your conversations with GovHub's built-in assistant. Customer Content often includes personal data about people other than you, such as your employees and teaming partners.

**Automatically, when you use the website or the app**

- Device and log data: IP address, browser, device type, pages and screens viewed, and timestamps.
- How you found us: the referring site, campaign tags (such as utm_source) and ad click identifiers (such as gclid), kept in our own cookies.
- Product events, such as starting a proposal or downloading a document. We record which action happened, not the text of your documents.

**From other sources**

- Public business records: SAM.gov entity and opportunity data, USAspending.gov award data, and the SBA's public certification search, including the business contact names and emails businesses choose to publish there.
- Public web pages, such as a company's own website and publicly available professional profiles.
- Email verification results from our verification provider, which tell us whether an address is likely to accept mail.

## 5. Cookies, analytics and advertising

We use our own cookies to keep you signed in, remember how you found us and measure our campaigns. We also use PostHog and Google Analytics for analytics, and the Google tag (Google Ads), the Meta Pixel and the LinkedIn Insight Tag to measure our advertising. When you sign up, Google Ads may receive a hashed (scrambled) copy of your email address to match the signup to an ad click.

Under California law, letting ad platforms collect data on our site for cross-context behavioral advertising can count as "sharing" personal information, even though no money changes hands. You can opt out: we honor the **Global Privacy Control** signal, and you can turn analytics and advertising tags off with the control on our [Cookie Notice](/cookies/), which lists every cookie and tag.

## 6. Email links and tracking

Some emails we send you directly, such as a reply to a message you sent us, contain links with a reference number for your record in our email system. It is not your email address. If you open such a link, we store the reference in a cookie (gh_lid) and attach it to your visits in our own analytics (PostHog), so we can see whether what we sent was useful. We remove it from the page address before any other analytics provider loads. Turning analytics off on the [Cookie Notice](/cookies/) removes it.

Account emails sent from the app, such as bid alerts and analysis results, record whether they were delivered, opened or clicked, so we can fix delivery problems and see which alerts are useful. Our cold outreach emails do not use open or click tracking.

## 7. If we contacted you about GovHub

We email people at businesses that bid on government contracts, and people in the government contracting community such as consultants, creators and associations, to tell them about GovHub. If you received one of these emails:

- **Where we got your details:** public business records (USAspending.gov, the SBA's certification search, SAM.gov), the business's own website, or public professional profiles and directories. We check that addresses are deliverable with an email verification provider.
- **What we keep:** your name, business email, job title, company, the public business information that led us to contact you, the emails we exchanged, and whether you replied.
- **How to stop it:** reply "no" or "unsubscribe" to any of our emails, or email [CONFIRM: monitored privacy address]. We add your address to a suppression list that applies to every GovHub sending address and campaign, and we act on your request within 10 business days.
- **Our legal basis**, where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, is our legitimate interest in telling businesses about a product relevant to their work. You can object at any time and we will stop.

GovHub sends outreach from govhub.online and from a set of related GovHub domains, all listed on our [company and domains page](/legal/). An email that claims to be from GovHub but comes from a domain not on that list is not from us.
