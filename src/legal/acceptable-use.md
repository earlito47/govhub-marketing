## 3. Automated access and customer-directed agents

### What counts as an agent

A "Customer-Directed Agent" is any autonomous or semi-autonomous software that acts in the GovHub application on someone's behalf: AI agents, bots, browser automation, scripts, macros and integrations. GovHub's own built-in assistant is a feature of the Services and is not a Customer-Directed Agent.

### When an agent is allowed

You may use a Customer-Directed Agent to access GovHub product functionality only:

- on behalf of an identified user who is an active paid subscriber, or who holds an authorized seat under an active paid organizational subscription;
- using that user's own access, not shared or pooled credentials;
- within the features, quotas and usage limits of that subscription; and
- through the GovHub web application, signed in as that user. GovHub does not offer a customer API or agent integration today. If we publish one, agents must use it in the way we document.

Free and trial accounts do not include agent access. A paid subscription is necessary for agent access, but it does not lift any other rule in the Terms or this Policy.

### What an agent may not do

A Customer-Directed Agent may not:

- access GovHub anonymously or on behalf of anyone who is not an identified paid subscriber or authorized paid seat;
- create accounts or start trials automatically, or access any account other than the identified user's;
- solve, avoid or disable CAPTCHAs, bot checks, authentication, rate limits, payment or authorization controls;
- impersonate a person or hide that it is automated where we ask it to identify itself;
- scrape or bulk-export Service content for redistribution or resale; or
- train or improve a competing product or model on content created by the Services, other than your own Customer Content and Output.

### Your responsibility

The account holder is responsible for every agent they authorize and for everything it does under their account, including charges, usage against plan limits and breaches of these rules, as if the account holder had acted directly. Keep your credentials and any tokens secure, and revoke an agent's access when you stop using it.

### What this section does not restrict

- **Assistive technology.** Screen readers, voice control, magnifiers and other accessibility tools are welcome within your own authorized access.
- **Your own security tools.** Password managers, endpoint security and similar tools your organization runs are permitted within your own authorized access.
- **Public pages.** Public pages on [www.govhub.online](/) may be read by automated tools in line with our [robots.txt](/robots.txt). That does not give access to the application or to any paid functionality.
