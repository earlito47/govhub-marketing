This Cookie Notice explains the cookies and similar technologies Linkshub Ltd. uses on GovHub's website ([www.govhub.online](/)) and app (app.govhub.online), and how to control them. It is part of our [Privacy Notice](/privacy/).

## Your controls

- **Turn analytics and advertising off.** Use the control on this page. It stops Google Analytics, PostHog, the Google tag (Google Ads), the Meta Pixel and the LinkedIn Insight Tag on both [www.govhub.online](/) and app.govhub.online, and removes the gh_lid email-link reference. It applies to this browser only and lasts a year, or until you clear your cookies.
- **Global Privacy Control.** If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we do not load the Google tag, the Meta Pixel or the LinkedIn Insight Tag, and we do not store email-link references. Google Analytics and PostHog still run unless you also turn analytics off here.
- **Your browser.** You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the cookies we need to sign you in, the app will not work.

If JavaScript is turned off, our pages cannot read your choice, and the Meta and LinkedIn image tags in the page can still load. Blocking third-party content in your browser stops them.

[CONFIRM: GovHub does not show a cookie consent banner. If GovHub markets to visitors in the UK or the EU, the analytics and advertising cookies below need prior consent there; add a banner that loads them only after consent.]

## Cookies we set ourselves

These are set on .govhub.online, so both the website and the app can read them.

| Name | Purpose | Lasts |
|---|---|---|
| gh_s | Identifies your current visit | 30 minutes without activity |
| gh_lt | Campaign tags (such as utm_source) for your current visit | 30 minutes |
| gh_ft | How you first found us: the referring site, campaign tags, ad click identifiers and first page | 90 days when set by the website, 2 years when set by the app [CONFIRM: align the two lifetimes] |
| gh_lv_www, gh_lv_app | Records that a visit's first page view was counted | The visit (website), 1 day (app) |
| gh_lid | Set only if you open a personal link in an email from us; see [section 6 of the Privacy Notice](/privacy/#6-email-links-and-tracking) | 90 days |
| gh_optout | Remembers that you turned analytics off | 1 year |

The app also keeps your sign-in session in your browser's local storage. It is needed for the app to work.

## Analytics and advertising technologies

| Provider | What it does | Cookies and storage | Stopped by the control on this page | Stopped by Global Privacy Control |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PostHog | Product and website analytics | ph_*_posthog cookie and local storage | Yes | No |
| Google Analytics 4 | Website and app analytics | _ga, _ga_* | Yes | No |
| Google tag (Google Ads) | Measures which ads lead to signups; may receive a hashed email address at signup | _gcl_* and related Google cookies | Yes | Yes |
| Meta Pixel | Measures our Facebook and Instagram ads and builds ad audiences | _fbp and Meta cookies | Yes | Yes |
| LinkedIn Insight Tag | Measures our LinkedIn ads and builds ad audiences | li_fat_id, bcookie and other LinkedIn cookies | Yes | Yes |
| Calendly (website only) | The "Book a demo" scheduling widget | Set by Calendly when you open the widget [CONFIRM] | No | No |

Each provider sets and controls its own cookies and their lifetimes, which can be up to two years. Session recording is off on the website. In the app, PostHog masks all page text and form inputs in anything it captures [CONFIRM: whether session recording is enabled in the app's PostHog project settings].

We also load fonts and a document viewer component from third-party content networks in the app, which receive your IP address when they serve those files. They do not set cookies for us.
